SHIPPING TERMS AND OPERATIONAL POLICIES
Carrier: Meridian Ocean Lines
Region: South Asia Export (Chennai, Nhava Sheva, Cochin)

BOOKING AND CUTOFF
Cargo cutoff: 3 working days before vessel ETD
Documentation cutoff (SI/VGM): 2 working days before vessel ETD
VGM (Verified Gross Mass) submission is mandatory under SOLAS regulations;
bookings without VGM will not be loaded.

CONTAINER TYPES OFFERED
20GP - 20ft General Purpose (standard dry container)
40GP - 40ft General Purpose
40HC - 40ft High Cube (extra 1ft internal height)
20RF / 40RF - Refrigerated containers (reefer), temperature range -25C to +25C
20OT - 20ft Open Top (for over-height cargo, on request only)

FREE TIME POLICY
Free time is the number of days a container may be held (at origin before
loading, or at destination after discharge) without incurring demurrage or
detention charges. Free time terms vary by trade lane and are specified in
the surcharge tariff for that lane. Extensions to free time require written
approval from the carrier's local agent at least 48 hours before expiry.

CFS STUFFING (LCL cargo only)
Container Freight Station (CFS) stuffing applies to Less-than-Container-Load
(LCL) shipments, where cargo is consolidated with other shippers' cargo at a
warehouse before loading. CFS stuffing charges are separate from ocean
freight and vary by weight/measurement (whichever is greater, W/M basis).

HAZARDOUS CARGO (IMO)
Hazardous cargo must be declared under the correct IMO class and packing
group. Carrier reserves the right to refuse hazardous bookings on lanes
where hazardous handling capacity is full. A separate hazardous surcharge
tariff applies and is not part of this general rate sheet.

CLAIMS AND LIABILITY
Carrier liability is governed by the Hague-Visby Rules unless the bill of
lading specifies otherwise. Claims must be filed within 9 months of
delivery (or the date delivery should have occurred).
